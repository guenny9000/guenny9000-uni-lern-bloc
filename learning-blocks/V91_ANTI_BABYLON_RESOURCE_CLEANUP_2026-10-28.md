# V91 Anti-Babylon: Resource and Cost Cleanup
Date: 2026-10-28
Status: DOCUMENTED_PROPOSAL — not a production activation.

## Objective
Reduce avoidable resource use, waste, duplicated administration, unnecessary transport, and lifecycle cost while protecting human dignity, essential services, nature, and lawful rights.

## Scope
- Materials and products: prevent waste first; maintain, repair, reuse, refurbish, recycle only after higher-value options.
- Energy and water: measure real consumption; prevent leaks and losses; protect watersheds, soil, and biodiversity.
- Food systems: reduce avoidable food loss; support local supply only where lifecycle costs and resource use justify it.
- Logistics: measure empty runs, duplicated routes, packaging, storage losses, and avoidable transport.
- Finance and procurement: expose total lifecycle cost, subsidies, maintenance, disposal, conflicts of interest, and verified savings.
- Administration: identify duplicate reporting, overlapping responsibilities, and processes with no demonstrated public benefit.
- Production: evaluate durability, repairability, spare parts, packaging, planned obsolescence, and end-of-life recovery.

## Required decision record
For each proposed measure record: baseline and source; scope and owner; full cost; expected benefit; environmental and social side effects; legal basis; measurable target; deadline; independent verification method; rollback or stop condition.

## Decision rules
1. CLAIM != EVIDENCE. No savings or environmental benefit is reported as achieved without measurements.
2. Prefer prevention, maintenance, repair, reuse, and simplification before new infrastructure.
3. New infrastructure is allowed when a documented need, lifecycle comparison, affordability, and ecological review support it.
4. No arbitrary blanket cuts to essential services, accessibility, safety, or rights.
5. No automatic penalties against people or organizations based on a heuristic marker alone.
6. Unknown data => UNKNOWN / REVIEW_REQUIRED, not a pass.
7. ISOLATE_NOT_DESTROY. Tests remain isolated; no external action is authorized by this document.
8. Request != authorization; detection != authorization; privileges_new <= privileges_old.
9. Recovery > optimization. Any pilot must be reversible and have a stop/rollback plan.
10. Minimize administrative burden: collect a metric once, reuse it lawfully, and remove duplicate reporting where safe.

## Minimum scorecard
Track, with units and dates: material input per delivered service; waste prevented; reuse/repair rate; energy and water consumption; avoidable transport and empty-run rate; lifecycle cost; administrative hours per case; service quality/accessibility; ecological impact; independently verified net savings.

## Implementation order
A. Inventory existing data and systems before buying or building anything.
B. Establish baselines and identify duplicate processes.
C. Rank changes by evidence, public benefit, lifecycle savings, ecological impact, and reversibility.
D. Run a small isolated pilot; test failure cases and unintended effects.
E. Publish results with source data, method, limitations, and review date.
F. Expand only after reproducible verification and authorized review.

## Evidence boundary
This file is a policy specification only. It does not prove that any municipality, company, repository, or live system has changed its behavior. It does not authorize production deployment, spending, deletion, enforcement, or external control.
